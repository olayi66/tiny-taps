# Tiny Taps ABC 123

A tap-to-learn alphabet and numbers app for infants and toddlers.

- One big letter at a time, with its name, its phonics sound, and two picture words
- Numbers 1–20 with counting
- Recorded teacher voices: American (Bella, Heart) or British (Emma)
- Three teaching styles: Calm teacher, Short & simple, Sing-song
- Installs to the home screen and works offline

Grown-ups: press and hold ⚙︎ to change accent, voice and style.
To install, open the site and tap **📲 Add Tiny Taps to this phone**.
